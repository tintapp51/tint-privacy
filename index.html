TINT Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

TINT ("the app") is made by vidy ("we", "us"). This policy explains what TINT collects, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it.

Questions or deletion requests: tintapp51@gmail.com

The short version

  • TINT blocks the apps you choose, during focus sessions and during the 7 Day Lock In.
  • To do that, TINT asks for two Android permissions that you turn on yourself: Usage access (to see which app is open) and Display over other apps (to show a block screen). TINT uses them only for blocking. Before it first asks, it explains why and asks you to agree.
  • TINT does not upload a list of the apps on your phone, and does not upload which apps you open. During a 7 Day Lock In it saves only counts for the apps you chose to block (see section 2).
  • TINT has no ads, and we do not sell your data.
  • Other TINT users can see your username, avatar, exams, streak and focus totals (section 4). They cannot see your email, goals or task names.
  • You can delete your account at any time, in the app or without it (section 7).

1. What TINT collects

Your account

  • Email address and password. If you use Google Sign-In, TINT receives your Google email address, account ID, name and profile picture link instead. Passwords are handled by our login provider and are never visible to us. We never receive your Google password.
  • Your username and avatar.
  • Sign-in records (time, IP address and device information), kept by our login provider for security.

What you set up in the app

  • The exams you are preparing for (only the preset exams are uploaded; a custom exam name you type under "Other" stays on your phone), your daily focus goal, and the goal and date you write during setup.
  • Your tasks, including their names, and a day-by-day history: task names, how many you completed, and a consistency score for each day.
  • Your focus sessions (date, start time, length and a session ID), and your totals for today, this week and all time.
  • Your streak, longest streak, last active date and total tasks completed.

Friends

  • Friend requests you send or receive. Friends are found by exact email address. The email is never shown to the person searching.

2. The 7 Day Lock In

If you start a 7 Day Lock In, TINT also saves:

  • The challenge (solo or squad), its code, the apps you chose to block, whether you are still in or have left, when you joined or left, and when your phone last checked in with our server.
  • How many times you tried to open each blocked app, per day.
  • For the apps you chose to block only: how many times you opened them, and for how many minutes, in the 7 days before the challenge started. TINT takes this once, when the challenge starts. It is used only for your end-of-challenge report.
  • How many emergency unlocks you used (5 minutes each, up to twice).

Only you can see your attempt counts, your before-numbers, your report and your emergency unlocks. Squad members cannot (section 4).

3. App blocking: permissions and background running

TINT uses these Android permissions:

  • Usage access: lets TINT see which app is open right now, so it can cover an app you chose to block. TINT also asks Android which app is your phone's home screen, only to tell the home screen apart from other apps. TINT does not upload a list of your apps and does not upload which apps you open.
  • Display over other apps: lets TINT show its block screen over a blocked app.
  • Foreground service and a notification: keeps blocking running. During a focus session this lasts for the session. During a 7 Day Lock In blocking runs continuously for the full 7 days, including when TINT is not open on screen. TINT shows a notification while blocking runs. On Android 13 and later, if you turn notifications off for TINT, Android hides this notification from your notification drawer, but TINT still appears in Android's list of active apps and blocking still runs. On Android 14 and later you may also be able to swipe the notification away.
  • Run at startup: if your phone restarts while a 7 Day Lock In is running or waiting to start, TINT restarts it. Focus sessions are not restarted after a restart. TINT also tries to resume a 7 Day Lock In if Android stops it.
  • Notifications: session and challenge reminders and a message when your challenge ends. These are scheduled on your phone.
  • Internet: for your account, sync, the leaderboard, friends and challenges.

You are asked first. Before TINT first asks you to turn on Usage access or Display over other apps, whether from the sign-up screen, the Focus tab or the 7 Day Lock In, it shows a screen that explains why TINT needs them and what data is used (and, for a 7 Day Lock In, what squad members can see), and asks you to agree. Choose Enable to continue to Android settings, or Not now to skip. Blocking cannot work without these two permissions. You can switch them off at any time in Android settings, and you can end a focus session, leave a challenge or delete your account at any time.

TINT does not use your microphone, camera, location or contacts.

What stays on your phone: which app is open right now (held only in memory, to decide whether to block), your blocking settings and the running state of a challenge, the list of apps you picked for focus sessions, how long you were away from TINT during a focus session, and any custom exam name.

4. Who can see what

Any signed-in TINT user can see your username, avatar, exams, streak, total tasks completed, focus minutes (today, this week and all time) and, for each day, how many tasks you completed out of how many (not what the tasks are). This appears on the leaderboard and in friend lists, even if you are not friends. It applies to every account, including accounts made through the 7 Day Lock In.

They cannot see your email, your written goal, your task names or your focus-session history.

Your squad members can see your username, avatar, the apps you chose to block, whether you are still in or have left, when you joined or left, and when your phone last checked in. They cannot see your blocked-attempt counts, your before-numbers, your report or your emergency unlocks.

Sharing: if you tap Share (for a squad invite or your recap or finish card), TINT hands the text or image to Android's share sheet. You choose where it goes. TINT does not send it anywhere itself.

5. How we use it, and who handles it

We use your information only to run TINT: your account, plans and progress, streaks, the leaderboard, friends, and the 7 Day Lock In and its report. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising. TINT has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

These providers handle data for us:

  • Supabase stores your account and app data. Its servers are in Seoul, South Korea.
  • Google, only if you choose Google Sign-In.
  • Expo delivers app updates. When TINT checks for an update, Expo receives your device's IP address, the app version, your device platform, the ID of the update installed, and a random ID the update system creates for this installation. This includes your IP address, but not your name or email.

6. Security

All data between TINT and our servers is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database access is restricted so that each account can read only its own private data.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data until you delete your account.

In the app: Profile, then Delete account. If you joined through the 7 Day Lock In, it is at the bottom of the challenge screen. Your login, profile, tasks, focus history, streaks, friend requests, challenge memberships and challenge statistics are deleted from our database straight away. TINT also stops blocking and clears its saved data on your phone.

Without the app: use our Delete your TINT account page, or email tintapp51@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my TINT account". We will delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

What may remain for a short time: if our hosting provider keeps backups, copies are overwritten on the provider's schedule. Our login provider may keep security logs for a limited time. We do not use these copies for anything else.

Squads: if you created a squad that has already started, it keeps running for the other members and your name is removed from its owner role. If it has not started, it closes.

8. Children

TINT is meant for people aged 13 and over (or the applicable minimum legal age for online services in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children under this age without parental consent.

Notice for Parents and Guardians: If you believe your child has created an account without your consent, please email us at tintapp51@gmail.com with the subject "Underage Account" and we will delete the account and its data promptly.
