TINT Privacy Policy

Delete your TINT account

This page explains how to delete your account and data for TINT, the app by vidy. You can do it inside the app, or without opening the app at all.

Option 1: inside the app

  1. Open TINT and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile, then tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm twice. Your account is deleted straight away.

If you joined through the 7 Day Lock In, scroll to the bottom of the challenge screen and tap Delete account.

Option 2: without the app

Email tintapp51@gmail.com from the email address on your TINT account, with the subject:

Delete my TINT account

If you signed in with Google, use your Google email address. Include your TINT username if you have one. We will delete your account within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your login, profile, tasks and task history, focus history, streaks, friend requests, 7 Day Lock In memberships and challenge statistics are deleted from our database. Deleting inside the app also stops blocking and clears TINT's saved data on your phone. If you ask by email, uninstalling TINT removes the data on your phone.

What may remain for a short time

  • Backups, if our hosting provider keeps any, until the provider overwrites them on its own schedule.
  • Security logs kept by our login provider, for a limited time.

We do not use these copies for anything else.

Squads

If you created a 7 Day Lock In squad that has already started, it keeps running for the other members. If it has not started yet, it closes.
