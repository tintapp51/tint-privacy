Delete Your TINT Account & Data

This page allows you to request the permanent deletion of your TINT account and all associated personal data in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.

What data will be deleted?

  • Your profile information (email address, username, avatar).
  • All task records, task history, and consistency scores.
  • Focus sessions, timers, and streak milestones.
  • Challenge memberships, 7 Day Lock In attempt counts, and squad records.

Note: Deletion from our primary database is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Immediate)

If you have the TINT app installed on your device:

  1. Open TINT and navigate to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm your choice. Your account and data are purged immediately.

Option 2: Web Deletion Request (Outside the App)

If you do not have the app installed or cannot sign in, send an account deletion request directly to our developer team:

  1. Send an email to: tintapp51@gmail.com
  2. Subject line: Delete my TINT account
  3. Send the email from the same email address associated with your TINT account so we can verify your identity.
